Small pill for labels, counts, and statuses. `add`/`del` tones reuse diff colors.

```jsx
<Badge tone="accent" dot>Claude</Badge>
<Badge tone="add">+12</Badge>
<Badge tone="del">−4</Badge>
<Badge tone="warn" variant="solid">2 warnings</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral | accent | add | del | warn | info`. `variant`: `soft` (tinted) or `solid` (filled). Sizes `sm | md`.
